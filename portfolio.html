Government of Makueni County

iGrid017 County Digital Superhighway

Applications Development team

Overview

iGrid017 is the county's integrated digital superhighway, developed and hosted in-house by the Applications Development team. The county ERP sits at its core, surrounded by shared platform services (identity and access, audit trail, notifications, reporting and system monitoring) and connected through a gateway to the departmental systems and public e-services listed below.

My contribution

My work includes backend services and data models for revenue billing and receipting, programme-based budget formulation and absorption tracking, and expenditure workflows from requisition through voucher and payment. I have also worked on REST API integrations with banks and payment gateways, as well as database queries used in cross-module reporting. I manage the county's cloud services.

County systems on iGrid017

Systems I built for the Government of Makueni County, connected to iGrid017.

Procurement

Procurement Portal

Vendors register as county suppliers and submit compliance documents online. The county publishes opportunities, receives bids and records transactions from award to completion. Supplier information and supporting documents require validation before review, and vendors must not be able to view other suppliers' records.

My role: I developed the supplier registration workflow, including category selection, document uploads, server-side validation and the review queue used by procurement officers. Access permissions restrict each vendor to their own submissions.
