Selected projects, in production.

Eighteen platforms carrying real workloads: county public financial management and e-procurement, project accountability, agriculture, education and asset registries, and private-sector ERP and inventory software. Each entry sets out the problem, the solution and my role in it, and the technology behind it.

Public finance
County government · 2023 to present

CIFMIS, County Integrated Financial Management

Government of Makueni County
Problem
County departments each plan budgets, commit spend and collect revenue, and all of it has to reconcile into one auditable picture for treasury and oversight bodies. Spreading that across disconnected records makes reporting slow and reconciliation manual.
Solution & role
Engineered and maintained the backend services and underlying data model for budgeting and expenditure workflows. Architected RESTful APIs integrating with banks and payment gateways so transactions flow in without re-keying, and optimised SQL queries to keep reporting responsive as the ledger grows.
Impact
Budget planning, commitments and revenue collection reconcile into a single auditable record for treasury and oversight bodies, with bank and gateway transactions captured automatically rather than re-entered by hand.
Technology
  • PHP
  • Laravel
  • MySQL
  • REST
  • Linux
  • Nginx
cifmis.makueni.go.ke
Procurement
County government · 2023 to present

Suppliers Registration Portal

Government of Makueni County
Problem
Any vendor bidding for county work must register and prequalify first. Handling that through physical paperwork limits who can realistically apply and slows procurement officers reviewing submissions.
Solution & role
Delivered the online registration path end to end: category selection, document upload, server-side validation and a review queue procurement staff work through. Access is scoped so vendors only ever see their own submission.
Impact
Vendor registration and prequalification run online rather than on paper, widening the pool that can realistically apply and giving procurement officers one queue to assess submissions in.
Technology
  • PHP
  • Laravel
  • MySQL
  • File handling
  • AuthN/AuthZ
procurement.makueni.go.ke
Accountability
County government · 2023 to present

PMTS, Projects Monitoring & Tracking

Government of Makueni County
Problem
Development projects run across every ward and department, and citizens have a legitimate claim on knowing what was promised, what it cost and how far it has got. Without a shared register that question is answered department by department.
Solution & role
Engineered the platform the county uses to publish project status openly, described on the system itself as improving efficiency, transparency and accountability in tracking projects across the county. Work covered the project data model, status workflows and the public-facing views.
Impact
Development projects across every ward and department report into one public register, so what was promised, what it cost and how far it has progressed is answerable from a single source rather than department by department.

Makueni County Sports Register

Makueni County Directorate of Sports
Problem
Teams, fixtures, results and playing grounds were tracked informally, leaving the directorate without a dependable picture of participation when allocating facilities and funding.
