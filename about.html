About

My Engineering Journey

My career began in graphic design at a printing company in Machakos before I transitioned into software engineering in 2018 at DevLan Solutions. There, I developed REST APIs and helped introduce version control, code reviews and unit testing into the team's development workflow. I subsequently developed web applications at Ezana Group, supported and extended an ERP system at Ukamba Bible College, and built native iOS and Android applications for World Christian Fellowship.

In 2022, I joined the ICT Authority as an ICT Officer based at the Machakos County headquarters. My responsibilities included network and end-user support, system audits and the implementation of security controls to protect information systems against unauthorised access and data breaches.

Current Role

Since March 2023, I have worked as a Software Engineer in the Applications Development section at the Government of Makueni County. Our team develops, maintains and hosts county applications in-house, contributing to iGrid017, the county's integrated digital superhighway. My responsibilities include backend development, enterprise systems integration and the management of cloud services supporting the county's applications.

Technical Focus

  • Backend Engineering: Development and maintenance of ERP modules, backend services and REST APIs using PHP and Laravel, with MySQL for data storage.
  • Systems Integration: Integration with banks, payment gateways, SMS providers and email services to support financial and operational workflows.
  • Database Engineering: Database schema design, query optimisation and reporting to support data integrity and reliable access to operational information.
  • Cloud and Infrastructure: Management of cloud services, Linux-based environments and Nginx application hosting.
  • Cybersecurity and Digital Forensics: Secure software development, access control, server-side validation and system auditing, informed by my academic background in Cyber Security and Digital Forensics.

Engineering Approach

I approach software development with particular attention to data integrity, access control and the lifecycle of business records. For systems involving financial transactions and official records, I consider the data model, validation rules and workflow states early in the development process, before building the user interface.

I implement server-side validation and role-based access controls appropriate to each application's requirements. I also pay close attention to database structure and query performance to keep reporting practical as data volumes grow.

My development workflow emphasises version control, code review and testing to improve maintainability and reduce the risk of introducing defects. I work closely with operational teams to translate business requirements into software that supports established processes and can be maintained over time.
