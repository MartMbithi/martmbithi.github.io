// professional profile

Secure systems, engineered end to end.

Software engineer and systems builder with eight years converting tangled requirements into production software. I work across backend, frontend, cloud infrastructure and security, because a system only holds together when all four do.

I'm Martin Mbithi Nzilani, a software engineer specialising in enterprise application development, systems integration and secure cloud infrastructure. For eight years I have engineered and maintained the web, mobile and cloud systems that carry money, statutory records and public accountability, and therefore cannot quietly fall over. Thirteen of them are government platforms now in production, serving over 3,000 users across 11 county departments. That work spans full-stack development, Linux and Unix administration, cloud infrastructure and REST API integration, with a recurring theme of replacing manual routine with automated, measurable process.

I have delivered this inside government, an NGO, a college and private product teams. Public-sector engineering in particular teaches you to read a policy document and see the data model hiding inside it, and to build for auditors as well as users. I deliver in Agile teams, apply DevOps practice as a discipline rather than a label, and invest real effort in mentoring junior engineers.

I currently sit inside county digital transformation at the Government of Makueni County, engineering financial and citizen-facing platforms and integrating them with banks and payment gateways. An MSc in Cyber Security and Digital Forensics underpins that work, keeping information security in front of me at every layer of a system rather than bolted on at the end.
